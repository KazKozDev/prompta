Chapter 2: How AI Understands Prompts
To use AI effectively, it helps to understand how it processes information.
Large Language Models (LLMs) are trained on enormous collections of text. During
training, they learn patterns in language and use those patterns to predict likely responses.
AI does not think like humans.
Instead, it analyzes:
● Words
● Context
● Relationships between ideas
● Patterns from training data
When you submit a prompt, the model examines the context and predicts the most
appropriate response.
For example:
Prompt:
"What are three benefits of exercise?"
Likely Response:
1. Improved health
2. Increased energy
3. Better mental well-being
The AI generates this answer because these ideas frequently appear together in its
training data.
Understanding this principle helps users create prompts that guide the AI toward better
outcomes.

Chapter 9: Common Prompting Mistakes
Many users receive poor results because of weak prompts.
Common mistakes include:

Being Too Vague
Bad:
"Write something about education."
Better:
"Write a 700-word article discussing the benefits of online learning for adult students."

Providing No Context
Bad:
"Create a plan."
Better:
"Create a six-month marketing plan for a small local bakery."

Conflicting Instructions
Bad:
"Write a detailed report in 50 words."
